## Implications

There are two measured effects: multiplier-tagged matches dominate recent net pool growth, while performance allocation benefits the high starting-rating cohort and penalizes the low starting-rating cohort relative to equal shares. Reducing K scales overall movement; narrowing performance bounds targets direct redistribution. Neither establishes or guarantees improved match closeness. Inspect bonus policy and preview configuration changes against historical data before making a blanket rating deduction.
