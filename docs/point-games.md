# Discord point games

Point games use the same available points as match predictions; they do not affect Elo or prediction stake progression. Commands and results are public in the server, including wager and player names; account balances remain private through `!bank`. Stakes are whole numbers from 1 to 100 points.

Set `CASINO_CHANNEL_ID` to the Discord channel ID where `!roulette`, `!coinflip`, `!baccarat`, `!craps`, and `!duel` should work. These commands are silently ignored in other channels and when the setting is missing. `!give`, `!bank`, and match prediction bets are unaffected. Duel buttons on already-posted challenges continue working until they expire.

- `!roulette <red|black|odd|even|low|high|0-36> <points>` spins a single-zero wheel. Even-money selections return twice the stake on a win; an exact number returns 36 times the stake. Zero is green and loses every even-money bet. Players can spin again immediately.
- `!coinflip <heads|tails> <points>` returns twice the stake for a correct choice.
- `!baccarat <player|banker|tie> <points>` deals a fresh eight-deck punto banco hand with standard automatic third-card rules. A player win returns twice the stake; a banker win returns the stake plus 95% profit, rounded down to whole points; a correct tie bet returns nine times the stake. On a tie, player and banker bets are refunded. Each return includes the stake.
- `!craps join <pass|dont> <points>` starts or joins a shared round, with one bet per player. The first bettor is the shooter and uses the Roll Dice button; `!craps status` retrieves the active table and a fresh button. Bets are held immediately and are visible in `!bank`. Betting closes on the first roll. On the come-out roll, 7 or 11 wins pass, 2 or 3 wins don't pass, and 12 loses pass but refunds don't pass. Otherwise the roll sets the point: rolling that point again wins pass, while 7 wins don't pass. Winning bets return twice the stake. The shooter can use `!craps cancel` before the first roll to refund all bets. After 10 minutes without a roll, any bettor can take over by pressing Roll Dice. Active rounds and bets survive restarts; while games are paused, no new bets are accepted but existing rounds can finish or be cancelled.
- `!craps take dont` removes the caller's active don't-pass wager and returns its stake. A pass-line bet cannot be taken down individually; the shooter can cancel the table before the first roll.
- Once a point is set, `!craps place <4|5|6|8|9|10> <points>` places a separate bet on a number; players can cover multiple numbers, with one active wager per player per number. A hit pays profit of 9:5 on 4 or 10, 7:5 on 5 or 9, and 7:6 on 6 or 8, rounded down to whole points. The stake stays held and can win repeatedly. A 7 after a point loses all place bets. They carry over after a point win and are off on the next come-out roll, including a 7. `!craps take <number>` returns that stake between rolls, including while paused. The shooter can cancel a carried table before its come-out roll and refund all held bets. `!craps status [page]` shows active bets in pages of ten. If betting is disabled, keep point games enabled until outstanding bets are resolved.
- `!duel @player <points>` offers an equal-stake challenge. Only the named player can use the **Accept Duel** or **Decline Duel** buttons. Declining closes the challenge without moving points. Challenges expire after two minutes, do not reserve points, and require both players to have sufficient available points on acceptance. The winner is selected at random and gains the opponent's stake. Older challenges can still be accepted with `!duel accept <challenge message ID>` until they expire.
- `!give @player <points>` transfers a positive whole number of available points to another player. There is no 100-point game stake limit for gifts; the sender must have enough available points. The transfer is public, but balances remain private. A new recipient receives the usual one-time 1,000-point account grant as well as the gift.
- `!refund <Discord ID|@player> <points> <reason>` adds a positive whole number of points to a player without using the owner's balance. Only the Discord ID configured in `PREDICTIONS_OWNER_ID` can use it; if that setting is empty, the command is disabled. The reason and owner are recorded in the prediction ledger, and the usual one-time 1,000-point grant applies to a new account. Refunds affect the shared casino and prediction balance.

Set `POINT_GAMES_ENABLED=1` to enable games independently, or `POINT_GAMES_ENABLED=0` to disable them. If unset, it follows `PREDICTIONS_ENABLED` (which defaults off). The existing `!pred pause` also stops new game plays and duel acceptances until resumed. Point transfers, payouts, house funding and collections are recorded atomically in the prediction ledger. Operation IDs prevent repeat processing of the same Discord message.

The prediction owner can use `!pred house` for a private all-time breakdown of roulette, coin flip, baccarat, and craps house collections, funding and net points. Duels and gifts are excluded because their points move between players rather than to or from the house.

### Full craps table commands and rules

All wagers use whole points; each individual wager is limited to 1–100 points. The shared table accepts bets from multiple players. The first line bettor is the shooter. `!craps status [page]` shows active wagers in pages of ten, and the roll button publishes a separate result for each roll. Only the shooter can roll until ten minutes have elapsed without a roll, when any player with an active wager can take over.

- `!craps come <points>` and `!craps dontcome <points>` are available after the table point is set. The next roll settles the bet on 7, 11, 2, 3 or 12; otherwise it moves the bet to its own number. Come wins on 7 or 11 and loses on 2, 3 or 12; don't come loses on 7 or 11, wins on 2 or 3 and pushes on 12. Once moved, a come bet wins when its number rolls before 7; a don't come bet wins on 7 before its number. These bets can persist across line rounds. `!craps take dontcome <number>` removes one of your don't come bets and refunds its odds as well. A pending don't come may be removed with `!craps take dontcome`. A come bet is a contract bet.
- `!craps odds <pass|dont> <points>` places odds behind your established line bet. `!craps odds <come|dontcome> <number> <points>` places odds behind your established come or don't come bet. One odds wager is allowed per type and number. Odds pay true odds: pass/come 2:1 on 4/10, 3:2 on 5/9, 6:5 on 6/8; don't odds pay the inverse. Fractional profit is rounded down. `!craps take odds <pass|dont>`, or `!craps take odds <come|dontcome> <number>`, takes down odds. Taking down a don't flat bet also returns its associated odds. Come odds are automatically off on come-out rolls; other odds work automatically. If the flat bet resolves while its odds are off, the odds stake is returned.
- `!craps hardway <4|6|8|10> <points>` bets on doubles before an easy version of that number or 7. The aliases 22, 33, 44, and 55 also work. A hard 4/10 pays 7:1 profit and a hard 6/8 pays 9:1; a winning bet remains up. `!craps take hardway <number>` takes it down. Hardways are automatically off on come-out rolls.
- `!craps lay <4|5|6|8|9|10> <points>` wins if 7 rolls before the selected number. On a win, the bet returns the stake plus true-odds profit (1:2 on 4/10, 2:3 on 5/9, 5:6 on 6/8), less a 5% commission on the rounded-down profit, rounded up to a whole point. Lay bets work on come-out rolls by default. `!craps take lay <number>` returns the wager.
- `!craps field <points>` is a one-roll bet: 2 and 12 pay 2:1 profit; 3, 4, 9, 10 and 11 pay 1:1; other totals lose. `!craps prop <2|3|11|12> <points>` is a one-roll bet on that total, paying 30:1 profit on 2 or 12 and 15:1 on 3 or 11. `!craps anycraps <points>` wins on 2, 3 or 12 and pays 7:1 profit. `!craps take field`, `!craps take anycraps`, or `!craps take prop <number>` removes one before it rolls.
- `!craps on <place|hardway|lay> <number>`, `!craps off <place|hardway|lay> <number>`, and `!craps auto <place|hardway|lay> <number>` control whether your wager works. Use `!craps on odds <pass|dont|come|dontcome> <number>` (or `off`/`auto`) to control odds. `auto` restores the defaults above. Pass, come, don't pass and don't come flat bets are not switchable: they follow their own rules. A shooter cannot cancel an open table while other active wagers remain; those wagers must be resolved or taken down as allowed.

`!craps table` posts a reusable shared betting panel. Choose a specific bet from a menu and enter the number of points in the popup; the bet is placed for the player who submitted it. The table must still be on the same roll when the popup is submitted. The panel's Roll Dice button respects the shooter and idle-takeover rules; My Bets privately shows up to 25 of the caller's active wagers. Taking down bets and changing working states remain available through the commands above.

- `!craps horn <points>` splits a one-roll stake equally across 2, 3, 11, and 12. The stake must be a multiple of 4. A 2 or 12 returns 31 times one quarter of the stake; a 3 or 11 returns 16 times one quarter. The other three portions lose. `!craps take horn` removes a horn wager before the roll.
- `!craps fire <points>` wagers on the shooter making different point numbers in one hand. Place it before the first point is established; a player may have one active fire wager. It follows the hand across come-out rolls, and a come-out 7 does not end it. Repeated points count only once. On a seven-out, fewer than four unique points loses; four pays 24:1 profit, five pays 249:1. Making all six pays 999:1 immediately. `!craps take fire` is available before the first point, after which the wager is locked. If an idle-table takeover occurs, the hand and its fire bets continue. A new hand begins after a seven-out; the bot may retain the same shooter. For an active table that predates fire-bet tracking, fire wagers remain unavailable until the next seven-out because the earlier point history is unknown. Fire progress is shown on the roll results and table status.

Horn and fire wagers are also available from `!craps table` in the field and proposition menu. Each wager is limited to 100 points; a horn stake must be between 4 and 100 and divisible by four.
